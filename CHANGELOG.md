# Changelog

## v1.0 – 2026-11-05 – first public release
- Map sector by centre + size or four coordinates; presets up to 32 × 32 km; UTM grid
- Heightmap from AWS terrain tiles, local DEM or elevation WMS; .asc, 16-bit PNG/RAW, R32, GeoTIFF
- Satellite map from Sentinel-2 cloudless, OpenTopoMap or your own XYZ / WMS
- OpenStreetMap shapefiles with unique place-based names, vertex editing and custom drawing
- 19 special zone types (craters, trenches, berms, dams, quarries, dunes, terraces …)
- Heightmap morphing for water, roads, buildings and flatten areas
- Surface masks, splat masks, RGBA splatmap, Enfusion block check
- Engine profiles for Arma Reforger, Arma 3, Unreal and Unity
- `ATTRIBUTION.txt` in every export; credits & licences in the manual
- Only key-free services; coordinate search; 12 languages
