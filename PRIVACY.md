# Privacy

**Short version:** GTG Converter has no backend, no account, no analytics and no cookies. Everything runs in your browser.

## What stays on your device
- Settings, custom shapes and the last session are stored in your browser (`localStorage`, IndexedDB).
- Downloaded map tiles and OpenStreetMap scans are cached in your browser to reduce traffic.
- Exports are created locally and saved by your browser.

Clear them at any time in your browser settings (site data) or in the tool's data-cache panel.

## Which services your browser contacts
Your browser connects directly to these services. They receive your IP address and normal browser data (user agent, referrer). Their own privacy policies apply.

| Purpose | Service |
|---|---|
| Hosting of the tool | GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.) |
| Libraries | unpkg.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net, cdnjs.cloudflare.com |
| Fonts | Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) |
| OpenStreetMap data | overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems |
| Elevation | s3.amazonaws.com (AWS Open Data) |
| Imagery / map backgrounds | tiles.maps.eox.at, tile.opentopomap.org, tile.openstreetmap.org |
| Your own XYZ / WMS (only if you set one) | the server you entered |
